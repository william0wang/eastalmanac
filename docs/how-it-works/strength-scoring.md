# How We Score Five-Element Strength: The Complete Weight Tables

_Last verified: 2026-10_

## TL;DR
Most fortune-telling applications treat Five-Element strength as an opaque "black box." EastAlmanac scores charts using a fully deterministic mathematical model rooted in classical Ziping BaZi (*Yuan Hai Zi Ping* 《渊海子平》): transparent heavenly stems and hidden branch stems are assigned fixed base weights, multiplied by the seasonal commander's prosperity factor (旺相休囚死), and summed into five elemental totals. When the combined share of the Day Master and its supporting Seal reaches 50% or higher, the chart is classified as **Strong** (身强); otherwise, it is **Weak** (身弱). Favorable and unfavorable elements (喜用神 / 忌神) are then derived via the classical balancing method (扶抑法).

---

## 1. Input and Output Specification

### Input
A validated Four Pillars chart comprising:
- Four heavenly stems (Year, Month, Day, Hour) and their elemental affiliations.
- Four earthly branches with their hidden stems categorized into **Main Qi** (本气/主气), **Middle Qi** (中气), and **Residual Qi** (余气).
- Identification of the Day Master (the Day stem) and the Month Commander (the Month branch's main element).

### Output
- Exact numeric scores and chart percentage shares for Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water.
- Day Master classification: `strong` or `weak`, accompanied by its exact support share.
- Ordered favorable elements: Primary Remedy (用神) followed by Supporting Remedy (喜神).
- Unfavorable elements (忌神) that aggravate energetic imbalance.

## 2. Complete Mathematical Model

### A. Base Positional Weights
In classical BaZi theory (*得地* and *得势*), energy manifest in the heavenly stems is outward and active, whereas energy concealed within the earthly branches represents latent reservoir qi:

| Role | Placement | Base Weight | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Transparent Stem** (透干) | Any of the 4 Heavenly Stems | **100** | Full manifestation in the outer pillar |
| **Main Hidden Qi** (本气/主气) | Earthly Branch dominant stem | **60** | Deepest, prevailing energetic root |
| **Middle Hidden Qi** (中气) | Earthly Branch secondary stem | **30** | Associated seasonal or elemental storage qi |
| **Residual Hidden Qi** (余气) | Earthly Branch tertiary stem | **15** | Lingering trace qi from preceding transitions |

> **Note on Day Stem**: The Day Master stem itself is manifest in the physical chart and receives the standard transparent stem weight of 100 before seasonal modulation.

---

### B. Seasonal Modulation Multipliers (得令: 旺相休囚死)
The Month Branch (月令) dictates the climate and governing element of the chart (*"以月令为纲"*). Every stem—both manifest and hidden—is evaluated against the Month Commander element:

| Seasonal State | Classical Definition | Multiplier | Energy Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prosperous (旺)** | Same element as the Month Commander | **1.5** | Peak seasonal reign; at maximum vitality |
| **Strong (相)** | Element generated by the Month Commander | **1.2** | Actively nourished by the season |
| **Resting (休)** | Element that generates the Month Commander | **1.0** | Mother element; spent vitality from birthing the season |
| **Imprisoned (囚)** | Element that controls the Month Commander | **0.8** | Suppressed by the overwhelming seasonal mandate |
| **Dead (死)** | Element controlled by the Month Commander | **0.6** | Subdued and depleted under seasonal domination |

---

### C. Total Score Formulation

For each of the five elements (`wood`, `fire`, `earth`, `metal`, `water`):

```
elementScore = Σ (transparent stems of that element)   stemWeight × seasonFactor(element, monthCommander)
             + Σ (hidden stems of that element)        hiddenWeight(role) × seasonFactor(element, monthCommander)
```

Where:
- `monthCommander` — the commanding element of the month branch (月令主气五行).
- `seasonFactor(element, monthCommander)` — the seasonal multiplier from Table B (旺相休囚死).
- `stemWeight` = 100; `hiddenWeight(role)` ∈ {60, 30, 15} by role, from Table A.
- `totalChartScore` — the sum of all five `elementScore` values.
- `elementShare` = `elementScore / totalChartScore`.

---

### D. Day Master Status Determination (身强 vs. 身弱)
The model groups elements into two opposing energetic camps:
1. **Supporting Party (印比党)**: The Day Master's own element (Peer / 比劫) + the element that generates it (Seal / 印星).
2. **Consuming Party (克泄耗党)**: The element that controls it (Officer / 官杀) + the element it generates (Food / 食伤) + the element it controls (Wealth / 财星).

```
supportShare = (elementScore(dayMaster) + elementScore(sealElement)) / totalChartScore
```

- If `supportShare` ≥ 0.50 (50%), the chart is **Strong (身强)**.
- If `supportShare` < 0.50, the chart is **Weak (身弱)**.

---

### E. Favorable and Unfavorable Elements (Mainstream 扶抑法)

The goal of the balancing method is dynamic equilibrium (*中和为贵*):

#### Case 1: Weak Day Master (身弱)
The self lacks sufficient vitality and requires strengthening:
- **Primary Remedy (用神)**: **Seal (印星, 生我)** — Mother element that directly nurtures the Day Master.
- **Supporting Remedy (喜神)**: **Peer (比劫, 同我)** — Reinforces the self through companionship and alliance.
- **Unfavorable (忌神)**: **Officer (官杀, 克我)**, **Food (食伤, 泄我)**, and **Wealth (财星, 耗我)**.

#### Case 2: Strong Day Master (身强)
The self has surplus vitality and must discharge or discipline excess force:
- **Primary Remedy (用神)**: **Officer (官杀, 克我)** — Establishes discipline, order, and purpose.
- **Supporting Remedy (喜神)**: **Food (食伤, 我生)** — Drains excess force into creative output; and **Wealth (财星, 我克)** — Consumes surplus strength into achievement.
- **Unfavorable (忌神)**: **Seal (印星, 生我)** and **Peer (比劫, 同我)**.

## 3. Worked Golden Example

Consider a male born **1990-01-15 14:30 (+08:00)**:

### Chart Setup
- **Year Pillar**: 己巳 (Ji Si) — Stem: 己 (Earth); Branch: 巳 (Fire; Hidden: 丙 Fire main 60, 庚 Metal middle 30, 戊 Earth residual 15)
- **Month Pillar**: 丁丑 (Ding Chou) — Stem: 丁 (Fire); Branch: 丑 (Earth; Hidden: 己 Earth main 60, 癸 Water middle 30, 辛 Metal residual 15). **Month Commander = Earth**.
- **Day Pillar**: 庚辰 (Geng Chen) — Stem: 庚 (Metal, **Day Master**); Branch: 辰 (Earth; Hidden: 戊 Earth main 60, 乙 Wood middle 30, 癸 Water residual 15)
- **Hour Pillar**: 癸未 (Gui Wei) — Stem: 癸 (Water); Branch: 未 (Earth; Hidden: 己 Earth main 60, 丁 Fire middle 30, 乙 Wood residual 15)

### Seasonal Multipliers (Month Commander = Earth)
- Earth: Prosperous (旺) $\times 1.5$
- Metal: Strong (相) $\times 1.2$
- Fire: Resting (休) $\times 1.0$
- Wood: Imprisoned (囚) $\times 0.8$
- Water: Dead (死) $\times 0.6$

### Elemental Calculation
1. **Wood**:
   - Hidden 乙 in 辰 (middle 30): $30 \times 0.8 = 24.0$
   - Hidden 乙 in 未 (residual 15): $15 \times 0.8 = 12.0$
   - **Total Wood Score = 36.0** (Share: 3.87%)

2. **Fire**:
   - Stem 丁: $100 \times 1.0 = 100.0$
   - Hidden 丙 in 巳 (main 60): $60 \times 1.0 = 60.0$
   - Hidden 丁 in 未 (middle 30): $30 \times 1.0 = 30.0$
   - **Total Fire Score = 190.0** (Share: 20.44%)

3. **Earth**:
   - Stem 己: $100 \times 1.5 = 150.0$
   - Hidden 戊 in 巳 (residual 15): $15 \times 1.5 = 22.5$
   - Hidden 己 in 丑 (main 60): $60 \times 1.5 = 90.0$
   - Hidden 戊 in 辰 (main 60): $60 \times 1.5 = 90.0$
   - Hidden 己 in 未 (main 60): $60 \times 1.5 = 90.0$
   - **Total Earth Score = 442.5** (Share: 47.61%)

4. **Metal**:
   - Stem 庚 (Day Master): $100 \times 1.2 = 120.0$
   - Hidden 庚 in 巳 (middle 30): $30 \times 1.2 = 36.0$
   - Hidden 辛 in 丑 (residual 15): $15 \times 1.2 = 18.0$
   - **Total Metal Score = 174.0** (Share: 18.72%)

5. **Water**:
   - Stem 癸: $100 \times 0.6 = 60.0$
   - Hidden 癸 in 丑 (middle 30) & 辰 (residual 15): $(30 + 15) \times 0.6 = 27.0$
   - **Total Water Score = 87.0** (Share: 9.36%)

### Evaluation
- **Total Chart Score**: $36.0 + 190.0 + 442.5 + 174.0 + 87.0 = \mathbf{929.5}$
- **Day Master (Metal) + Seal (Earth)**: $174.0 + 442.5 = 616.5$
- **Support Share**: $\frac{616.5}{929.5} \approx \mathbf{66.33\%}$
- Since $66.33\% \ge 50\%$, the Day Master is **Strong (身强)**.
- **Favorable Elements (喜用神)**:
  - Primary Remedy (用神): **Fire** (Officer / 官杀, 克我)
  - Supporting Remedies (喜神): **Water** (Food / 食伤, 我生) and **Wood** (Wealth / 财星, 我克)
- **Unfavorable Elements (忌神)**: **Earth** (Seal / 印星) and **Metal** (Peer / 比劫)

## 4. Architectural Boundaries and Non-Covered Special Structures

To maintain mathematical determinism, transparent auditing, and low misclassification risk, this calculation layer deliberately implements the **pure mainstream balancing method (扶抑法)** and sets clear boundaries:

1. **Follow Structures (从格)**: In charts where the Day Master possesses virtually zero root support, classical theory dictates "abandoning the self" to follow the dominant force (Follow-Wealth, Follow-Officer, etc.). Detecting true vs. false surrender (真从 vs. 假从) remains one of the most contentious subjects among traditional masters. Hardcoding rigid heuristics risks complete inversion of remedies; such extreme cases are left to downstream contextual interpretation.
2. **Dominant Pure Structures (专旺格)**: Charts overwhelmingly saturated by a single element (e.g., Yanxiang for Fire, Quzhi for Wood) reject suppression and favor smooth outflow. These represent less than 3% of charts and are treated with nuance in reading frameworks rather than forced into the general calculator.
3. **Climate Temperature Remedies (调候)**: Classical texts (*Qiong Tong Bao Jian* 《穷通宝鉴》) prioritize winter warming (Fire) and summer cooling (Water) over mechanical strength scores. Future iterations plan to introduce climate recommendations as non-breaking auxiliary layers.
